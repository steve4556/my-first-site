🍽️ French-Scalloped Potatoes

Layers of thinly sliced potatoes and tender onions baked in a rich, thyme-infused garlic cream sauce, topped with bubbly mozzarella cheese.

Prep Time Cook Time Servings
20 mins 55 mins 4 people

Calorie & Nutrient Breakdown (Per Serving)

Calories Protein Carbs Fats
260 kcal 6g 20g 18g

Nutritional Highlights

*Mineral-Dense Energy Base
Thinly sliced medium potatoes yield 20 grams of clean complex carbohydrates to replenish essential muscle fuel and provide daily potassium support.
*Thyme Immune Boost
Fragrant thyme leaves supply natural antioxidants and essential vitamins that enhance standard respiratory health and digestion.
*Mozzarella Calcium Infrastructure
A rich layer of bubbling mozzarella cheese yields 6 grams of complete protein alongside a heavy dose of bone-strengthening structural calcium.

Ingredients

  • 4 medium potatoes, peeled and sliced thinly
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 medium onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  • 2 cloves minced garlic
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp everything bagel seasoning
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 cup whipping cream
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (177°C). Grease a casserole dish thoroughly with butter.
  2. Arrange half of your thinly sliced potatoes evenly along the bottom of the dish, and dot them with small pieces of the butter.
  3. Top the potato layer completely with your thinly sliced onions.
  4. Sprinkle the thyme leaves, minced garlic, salt, and everything bagel seasoning uniformly over the onions.
  5. In a separate bowl or measuring cup, mix the whipping cream and cornstarch together until completely smooth. Pour this cream mixture evenly over the potato layers.
  6. Add half of the mozzarella cheese across the top, then cover it with your remaining sliced potatoes.
  7. Sprinkle the rest of the mozzarella cheese evenly over the final potato layer.
  8. Bake for 55 minutes until the center is set, the potatoes are tender, and the cheese top is beautifully lightly browned. Sprinkle with fresh parsley and enjoy!
