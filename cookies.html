🍪 Chocolate Chip Cookies

Classic, golden-brown cookies featuring crisp edges and a perfectly soft, chocolate-loaded center.

Prep TimeCook TimeServings

Nutritional Highlights

*Quick Energy Source
Provides 20 grams of easily digestible carbohydrates, making it an excellent fast-acting snack or sweet reward.
*Rich Molasses Notes
The inclusion of dark brown sugar brings trace essential minerals like potassium and calcium alongside its deep caramel flavor profile.
*Whole Egg Structure
Fresh whole eggs provide the critical structural proteins and lecithin required to bind the dough for a chewy bite.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/4 cups flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 cup butter
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 eggs
  • 12 oz chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C) and grease a baking tray.
  2. In a medium bowl, sift together the flour, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.
  3. In a separate large bowl, combine the butter, white sugar, brown sugar, and vanilla extract. Beat the mixture thoroughly until light and creamy.
  4. Slowly beat the eggs into the creamed sugar mixture, one at a time, blending well.
  5. Gradually add the flour mixture into the wet ingredients, stirring continuously, then gently fold in the chocolate chips.
  6. Roll the dough into even, bite-sized cookie dough balls and arrange them on your greased baking tray.
  7. Bake for 10 minutes, transfer to a wire rack to cool, and enjoy!
