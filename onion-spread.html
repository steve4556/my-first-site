🧅 Vidalia Onion Spread

A sweet, deeply caramelized roasted Vidalia onion and garlic spread blended with fresh chives, perfect for pairing with savory crisps and rich cheeses.

Prep Time Cook Time Servings
15 mins 40 mins 8 servings

Calorie & Nutrient Breakdown (Per Serving)

Calories Protein Carbs Fats
95 kcal 1g 12g 5g

Nutritional Highlights

*Allium Antioxidants
Sweet Vidalia onions and roasted garlic are rich in quercetin and allicin, compounds that support a healthy circulatory system and cellular defense.
*Healthy Monounsaturated Fats
A tablespoon of extra virgin olive oil provides clear, heart-healthy monounsaturated fats that aid absorption of essential fat-soluble nutrients.
*Natural Honey Sugars
A hint of pure honey lends natural sweetness to help balance the roasted flavor notes, delivering clean energy without processed high-fructose corn syrups.

Ingredients

  • 4 large sweet onions, quartered lengthwise
  • 12 large garlic cloves, peeled
  • 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil (EVOO)
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • Salt and pepper (S+P) to taste
  • 2 tbsp chopped chives

Serve with (Suggested):

  • Crostini's
  • Flat bread crisps
  • Blue cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Lightly oil a 13x9-inch baking dish.
  2. Toss the quartered sweet onions and peeled garlic cloves together directly in the prepared baking dish.
  3. In a small cup or bowl, whisk the extra virgin olive oil and honey together until smooth, then drizzle the mixture evenly over the onions and garlic.
  4. Season generously with salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Bake for 40 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the onions and garlic turn beautifully tender and golden.
  6. Remove from the oven and let the mixture cool completely.
  7. Transfer the cooled ingredients into a food processor and puree until entirely smooth and uniform.
  8. Add the chopped chives into the processor mix and fold or blend gently. Transfer to your serving dish, sprinkle with additional fresh chives before serving, and enjoy!
