🥢 Chicken and Vegetable Stir Fry

A healthy and flavorful dish that's quick to prepare and perfect for a busy weeknight.

Prep TimeCook TimeServings
10 mins10 mins4 people

Calorie & Nutrient Breakdown (Per Serving)

CaloriesProteinCarbsFats
380 kcal35g24g12g

Ingredients

  • 2 large chicken breasts, cubed
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 carrot, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

Instructions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat.
  2. Add the cubed chicken and cook for 5-6 minutes until browned and cooked through.
  3. Toss in the broccoli, bell pepper, and carrot slices. Stir frequently for 3-4 minutes.
  4. Pour the soy sauce over the mixture and stir well to coat everything evenly.
  5. Cook for 1 more minute until the vegetables are tender-crisp, then remove from heat and serve over rice!
