🍳 3-Egg Omelette

A simple and delicious breakfast option that's perfect for any time of day.

Prep TimeCook TimeServings
5 mins5 mins1 person

Nutritional Highlights

*Protein
Contains 18 grams of complete protein from three large eggs to keep you full and energized.
Contains 6 grams of protein from 1/4 cup of shredded cheddar cheese.
*Essential Vitamins
Rich in Vitamin D and B12, which are crucial for bone health and daily energy levels.
*Healthy Fats & Choline
The yolks supply healthy fats and choline, an important nutrient for brain health and memory.

Ingredients

  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 2 tablespoons shredded cheddar cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl, add a pinch of salt and pepper, and whisk until blended.
  2. Melt the butter in a non-stick skillet over medium-low heat.
  3. Pour in the eggs and let them cook undisturbed for 1 minute, then gently push the cooked edges toward the center.
  4. When the top is mostly set but still slightly wet, sprinkle the cheese over one half.
  5. Fold the omelet in half, slide it onto a plate, and serve hot!
