🍕 Pizza Loaves

A fun and crispy twist on pizza night featuring meat-stuffed sourdough baguettes baked to bubbly perfection.

Nutritional Highlights

*High-Protein Foundation
Packed with 52 grams of powerful lean protein from the extra lean ground beef to fuel muscle growth and strength.
*Sourdough Carbohydrates
An 8-oz sourdough baguette provides a more easily digestible grain option for crisp textures and energy replenishment.
*Dual-Cheese Calcium Boost
The combination of Parmesan and Mozzarella delivers rich savory fats and a healthy dose of bone-strengthening calcium.

Ingredients

  • 8-oz sourdough baguette
  • 1 1/4 lbs extra lean ground beef
  • 1 clove minced garlic
  • 2 tbsp minced onion
  • 1 8-oz can tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese
  • Dash of pepper (to taste)

Instructions

  1. Split your sourdough baguette loaf or loaves in half lengthwise. Carefully remove some of the center breading, leaving a 1/2-inch thick crust shell.
  2. Evenly pat the extra lean ground beef directly into the hollowed bread shells, pressing it all the way out to the crust edges.
  3. Place the meat-stuffed bread loaves onto a broiler pan and broil at 375°F (190°C) for 10 minutes.
  4. While the beef broils, combine the minced garlic, minced onion, tomato sauce, a dash of pepper, oregano, and Parmesan cheese in a bowl to build the sauce.
  5. Carefully remove the pan from the oven, spoon the seasoned tomato sauce evenly over the broiled meat layer, and return to the oven to broil for an additional 10 minutes.
  6. Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese evenly across the top and finish broiling for a final 3 minutes, or until the cheese is completely melted and bubbly. Enjoy!
