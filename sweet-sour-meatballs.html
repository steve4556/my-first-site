🧆 Sweet & Sour Meatballs

Savory chopmeat meatballs spiked with horseradish and Tabasco, baked tender and coated in a bold, rich homemade sweet and sour glaze.

Prep Time Bake Time Servings
20 mins 20 mins 4-6 people

Nutritional Highlights

*High-Density Complete Protein
One pound of quality chopmeat combined with two large eggs provides 26 grams of essential complete protein to build cellular tissue and keep you full.
*Glaze Carbohydrates
The tangy-sweet sauce blend utilizes brown sugar and tomato ketchup base ingredients to create high-yield energy carbohydrates for active glycogen loading.
*Allium & Horseradish Cleansing
Infusing minced onion and hot grated horseradish delivers strong compounds that support optimal circulatory flow, breathing clarity, and daily digestion paths.

Ingredients

For the Meatballs:

  • 1 lb. chopmeat
  • 3/4 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 1/2 tsp. minced onion
  • 1/2 tsp. horseradish
  • 3 drops Tabasco
  • 3/4 tsp. salt
  • 1/2 tsp. pepper
  • 2 eggs

For the Sauce:

  • 3/4 cup ketchup
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1/4 cup vinegar
  • 2 tbsp. brown sugar
  • 1 tsp. Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp. dry mustard
  • 1/2 tsp. salt
  • 1/4 tsp. pepper
  • 3 drops Tabasco

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (204°C).
  2. In a large bowl, combine all of your meatball ingredients (chopmeat, breadcrumbs, minced onion, horseradish, 3 drops of Tabasco, salt, pepper, and 2 eggs). Mix thoroughly and shape into even-sized balls.
  3. Arrange the meatballs onto a baking sheet and bake at 400°F for about 20 minutes until cooked through and nicely browned.
  4. While the meatballs are baking, combine all of your sauce ingredients (ketchup, water, vinegar, brown sugar, Worcestershire sauce, dry mustard, salt, pepper, and 3 drops of Tabasco) in a saucepan.
  5. Bring the sauce mixture to a simmer and cook over medium-low heat, stirring occasionally, until fully blended and slightly thickened.
  6. Toss the warm, freshly baked meatballs directly in the sweet and sour sauce glaze until beautifully coated, transfer to a platter, and serve hot!
