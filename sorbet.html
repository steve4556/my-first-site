🍧 Strawberry Mango Sorbet

A vibrant, icy-smooth summer refreshment exploding with naturally sweet mango cubes, zesty citrus juices, and frozen strawberries.

Prep Time Freeze Time Servings
10 mins 8 hours 4 people

Calorie & Nutrient Breakdown (Per Serving)

Calories Protein Carbs Fats
60 kcal 1g 15g 0g

Nutritional Highlights

*Zero-Fat Clean Energy
Yields 15 grams of clean fruit sugars from fresh mango and strawberries, giving you a quick boost without processing heavy refined oils.
*High-Density Vitamin C
A combination of fresh fruit and splashes of citrus juices packs a powerful punch of immune-boosting antioxidants.
*Pure Plant Hydration
Naturally high water content in strawberries supports total daily cellular hydration while satisfying sweet dessert cravings.

Ingredients

  • 1 large mango
  • 2 cups fresh strawberries, hulled and sliced
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 tbsp orange juice
  • 1/2 tsp lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Place your sliced strawberries and chopped mango pieces in a single even layer onto a tray or large plate.
  2. Cover the tray securely with plastic wrap and transfer to the freezer overnight (or for at least 8 hours).
  3. Transfer the frozen solid fruit into a high-powered blender and pulse to finely chop the pieces down.
  4. Pour in the orange juice, lemon juice, and vanilla extract over the top.
  5. Blend continuously on high speed until completely smooth, velvety, and creamy.
  6. Spoon out into a cold dessert container of your choice and enjoy immediately!
