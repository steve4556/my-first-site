🧀 Melted Cheese Hor D'oeuvres

A warm, savory, and incredibly easy party appetizer featuring sharp cheddar and Swiss cheese mixed with a tangy touch of mustard, broiled on crisp onion crackers.

Prep Time Cook Time Yield
10 mins 5 mins 3 Platters

Calorie & Nutrient Breakdown (Per Serving - Approx. 3 Crackers)

Calories Protein Carbs Fats
160 kcal 5g 10g 11g

Nutritional Highlights

*High-Yield Calcium Blend
The combination of authentic shredded Cheddar and Swiss cheese supplies a powerful dose of bone-strengthening calcium and complete proteins.
*Savory Lipids Casing
Real mayonnaise serves as an excellent binding base to create a rich, emulsified texture that keeps the cheese perfectly molten under intense broiler heat.
*Aromatic Glucosinolate Kick
Two teaspoons of yellow or Dijon mustard add trace quantities of mineral antioxidants to cut through the rich dairy fats and enhance digestion.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cheddar cheese, shredded
  • 1 cup Swiss cheese, shredded
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 teaspoons mustard
  • Onion crackers

Instructions

  1. In a medium mixing bowl, thoroughly mix the first 4 ingredients (cheddar, Swiss, mayonnaise, and mustard) together until completely smooth and combined.
  2. Arrange your onion crackers in a single layer across a large baking sheet or broiler pan.
  3. Spoon and spread a generous portion of the cheese mixture evenly onto each onion cracker.
  4. Place under your oven's broiler and broil for 3 to 5 minutes, keeping a close eye on them, until the cheese topping is completely melted, bubbling, and beautifully slightly brown.
  5. Arrange onto serving trays and serve warm! Makes about 3 platters.
