🟫 Double Chocolate Brownies

A rich, fudgy, and incredibly decadent treat packed with double the chocolate goodness.

Nutritional Highlights

*Double Chocolate Indulgence
Packed with 12 ounces of rich chocolate chips to deliver a powerful, mood-boosting dose of antioxidants from the cocoa.
*Energy-Dense Treat
Provides 28 grams of carbohydrates per serving, making it a perfect quick energy boost or dessert pairing.
*Fudge-Like Consistency
Real whole eggs and butter provide the healthy fat base required to give these brownies their signature dense, melt-in-your-mouth texture.

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup flour
  • 1/4 tsp baking soda
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/3 cup butter
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 2 tablespoons water
  • 12 oz chocolate chips (divided)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 eggs

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 325°F (163°C) and grease a 9" square baking pan.
  2. In a medium bowl, combine the flour, baking soda, and salt. Set this flour mixture aside.
  3. In a small saucepan, combine the butter, sugar, and water. Bring just to a boil, then remove immediately from the heat.
  4. Add half of the chocolate chips (6 oz) and all of the vanilla extract to the saucepan. Stir continuously until the mixture is fully melted and completely smooth.
  5. Pour the melted chocolate mixture into a large mixing bowl. Beat the eggs in one at a time, mixing thoroughly after each addition.
  6. Gently blend the flour mixture into the wet ingredients until just combined, then fold in the remaining half of the chocolate chips.
  7. Pour the batter evenly into your pre-greased 9" square baking pan.
  8. Bake for 30 minutes, let cool, slice, and serve!
