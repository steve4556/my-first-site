🥔 Rosemary Steak Fries

Thick-cut baking potatoes seasoned with aromatic rosemary, garlic, herbs, and a kick of crushed red pepper flakes, roasted to crisp perfection.

Prep Time Cook Time Servings
15 mins 40 mins 6 people

Calorie & Nutrient Breakdown (Per Serving)

Calories Protein Carbs Fats
190 kcal 3g 25g 10g

Nutritional Highlights

*Clean Complex Carbohydrates
Baking potatoes provide 25 grams of slow-burning complex carbs and essential potassium to replenish muscle glycogen stores and sustain stamina.
*Anti-Inflammatory Herb Infusion
Fresh chopped rosemary delivers potent antioxidants and carnosic acid, which support immune strength and circulatory health.
*Capsaicin Metabolism Boost
Crushed red pepper flakes inject a fiery kick of capsaicin to naturally stimulate thermogenesis and add deep savory warmth.

Ingredients

  • 4 baking potatoes (roughly 3lbs)
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt
  • 2 tbsp chopped rosemary
  • 1 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
  • 1 tsp garlic & herb seasoning

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 450°F (232°C). Spray a large baking tray generously with non-stick cooking spray.
  2. Wash and scrub the baking potatoes, then cut each potato lengthwise in half, and slice into 8 even wedge pieces. Place the wedges directly onto the prepared cooking tray.
  3. Drizzle the potato wedges evenly with the vegetable oil, ensuring all pieces are lightly coated.
  4. Sprinkle the kosher salt, chopped rosemary, crushed red pepper flakes, and garlic & herb seasoning uniformly over the potatoes. Toss gently to combine.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven for 30-40 minutes. Be sure to carefully flip the wedges over halfway through the cooking time to ensure they bake evenly and get crisp on all sides. Serve hot!
