🍞 Pumpkin Raisin Nut Bread

A deeply spiced autumn classic loaded with pure pumpkin, sweet plump raisins, and crunchy chopped pecans, baked into warm, dense golden loaves.

Prep Time Oven Temp Default Yield
20 mins 350°F (175°C) 2 Full-Size Loaves

Ingredients

Wet Section (Combine):

  • 1 cup vegetable oil
  • 2/3 cup water
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 cups pure pumpkin

Add-Ins (Fold In):

  • 1 1/2 cups raisins
  • 1 1/2 cups chopped pecans

Dry Section (Sift Together):

  • 3 1/2 cups flour, sifted
  • 1 tsp nutmeg
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp baking soda
  • 3 cups sugar

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Thoroughly grease and flour your chosen baking pans (2 full-size loaf pans, 7 mini loaf pans, or muffin tins).
  2. In a large bowl, combine all of your wet ingredients (1 cup vegetable oil, 2/3 cup water, 4 eggs, and 2 cups pumpkin). Whisk thoroughly until smooth.
  3. In a separate massive bowl, sift together all of your dry ingredients (3 1/2 cups sifted flour, 1 tsp nutmeg, 1 tsp cinnamon, 1 1/2 tsp salt, 2 tsp baking soda, and 3 cups sugar).
  4. Make a deep well right in the center of your dry ingredients mixture. Pour the combined wet ingredients into the well and stir gently until just combined.
  5. Gently fold in your 1 1/2 cups raisins and 1 1/2 cups chopped pecans until evenly distributed throughout the thick batter.
  6. Pour the batter evenly into your prepared greased/floured baking containers.
  7. Bake at 350°F according to your pan selection format listed in the matrix above (1 hour for full loafs, 40-45 mins for mini loafs, or 20-25 mins for muffins) until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out completely clean. Let cool and enjoy!
