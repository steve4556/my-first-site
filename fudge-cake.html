🎂 Wellesley Fudge Cake

A rich, traditional chocolate fudge layer cake topped with a velvety, smooth, homemade chocolate frosting.

Prep Time Cook Time Yield
25 mins 35 mins 2x 9-inch layers

Calorie & Nutrient Breakdown (Per Serving)

Calories Protein Carbs Fats
380 kcal 4g 54g 16g

Nutritional Highlights

*Authentic Cocoa Flavanols
Baker's Unsweetened Chocolate provides real, direct cocoa compounds that boost heart circulatory elements and memory focus performance metrics.
*Energy Carbohydrates
Swan's Down Cake Flour paired with fine granulated sugar delivers clean grain energy reserves to quickly replenish muscle glycogen loads.
*Lecithin Structural Emulsion
Fresh whole eggs provide dietary lecithin proteins required to create a moist, tight crumb structure and rich fudgy mouthfeel.

Ingredients

The Fudge Cake:

  • 4 squares Baker's Unsweetened Chocolate
  • 1/2 cup hot water
  • 1 3/4 cups sugar
  • 2 cups sifted Swans Down Cake Flour
  • 1 teaspoon soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup vegetable shortening (or butter/margarine)
  • 3 eggs
  • 3/4 cup milk (decrease to 2/3 cup if using butter or margarine)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla

Easy Chocolate Frosting:

  • 4 squares Baker's Unsweetened Chocolate (or 4 packets Baker's Redi-Blend)
  • 1/4 cup butter or margarine
  • 1 pound unsifted confectioners' sugar
  • 1/8 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla

Instructions

Baking the Cake:

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line the bottoms of two 9-inch layer pans with paper.
  2. Melt chocolate in hot water in a double boiler, stirring frequently. Add 1/2 cup of the sugar; heat and stir for 2 minutes. Cool mixture until lukewarm.
  3. Measure sifted flour, soda, and salt, and sift them together.
  4. Cream the shortening. Gradually add the remaining 1 1/4 cups sugar and cream together until light and fluffy. Add eggs, one at a time, beating thoroughly after each addition.
  5. Alternately add the flour mixture and milk, beating after each addition until smooth. Blend in the vanilla and the cooled chocolate mixture.
  6. Pour batter into prepared pans. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, or until the cake springs back when lightly pressed. Cool completely before frosting.

Making the Frosting:

  1. Melt chocolate and butter over hot water.
  2. In a separate bowl, combine the confectioners' sugar, salt, milk, and vanilla.
  3. Stir the melted chocolate mixture directly into the sugar mixture.
  4. Let the frosting stand, stirring occasionally, until it reaches a thick spreadable consistency. Makes enough for tops and sides of the two 9-inch layers!
