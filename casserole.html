🥘 Southern Chicken Casserole

A comforting, hearty southern classic featuring crisp bacon, tender chicken, and flavorful pan-browned potatoes.

Prep TimeCook TimeServings
20 mins40 mins4 people

Nutritional Highlights

*Lean Muscle Recovery
Packed with high-quality lean protein from 2-3 lbs of chicken breast to keep you satisfied and fuel muscle repair.
*Complex Carbohydrates
Medium fresh potatoes act as an excellent clean source of potassium and complex carbohydrates for sustained daily energy.
*Savory Fat Blend
Frying in authentic bacon fat provides rich flavor and essential dietary fats that keep the chicken remarkably juicy during the bake.

Ingredients

  • 2-3 lbs chicken breast, cut into pieces
  • 4 medium potatoes
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp everything bagel seasoning
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp Garlic and Herb seasoning
  • 1/2 cup chopped shallots or green onions
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Bacon (for frying and crisping)

Instructions

  1. In a large Dutch oven, fry the bacon over medium heat until perfectly crisp. Remove the bacon, leaving the rendered fat in the pot.
  2. Add the cut-up chicken breast pieces to the hot bacon fat, browning them thoroughly on all sides. As the pieces brown, transfer them into a 2-quart casserole dish.
  3. Cut your 4 medium potatoes into 3 pieces lengthwise. Place them directly into the remaining bacon fat.
  4. Brown the potato pieces beautifully on both sides, then remove and arrange them right on top of the chicken in the casserole dish.
  5. Layer the thinly sliced onions evenly over the top of the potatoes.
  6. Sprinkle your everything bagel seasoning, salt, garlic and herb seasoning, chopped shallots, and the crisped bacon across the top.
  7. Cover the casserole dish tightly and bake in a preheated oven at 350°F (175°C) for 40 minutes until tender and hot. Garnish with fresh chopped parsley before serving!
