🥓 Roasted Dates w/ Parm & Bacon

Sweet, jammy pitted dates stuffed with a rich blend of cream cheese and coarsely grated parmesan, wrapped in crispy bacon and roasted to perfection.

Prep Time Cook Time Yield

Nutritional Highlights

*Natural Potassium Matrix
Pitted dates serve as an excellent source of natural dietary fiber and potassium, providing sweet energy density balanced by dense savory fillings.
*Double-Dairy Protein Core
The blend of coarsely grated parmesan and room-temperature cream cheese yields bone-strengthening calcium and structured, high-yield healthy fats.
*Satiating Lipids Wrapper
Smoked bacon slices provide a crispy, savory exterior wrapper that helps trap moisture inside the dates during intense high-heat roasting cycles.

Ingredients

  • 1/4 lb parmesan, coarsely grated
  • 2 tbsp cream cheese, at room temperature
  • Pitted dates (18 or more)
  • 6 slices bacon (cut into 3 pieces each)

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (204°C). Place your empty baking sheet inside the oven to preheat it along with the oven structure.
  2. In a medium bowl, combine the coarsely grated parmesan and room-temperature cream cheese, mixing thoroughly until completely smooth.
  3. Slice each pitted date carefully along one side to create an opening.
  4. Stuff a portion of the cheese mixture inside each date, then wrap a piece of cut bacon securely around the outside, overlapping the ends.
  5. Secure the bacon casing tightly in place using a wooden toothpick.
  6. Carefully transfer the stuffed dates onto the preheated hot baking sheet.
  7. Roast for 15 to 20 minutes, or until the bacon exterior is completely rendered and crisp. Serve hot!

*Note: You may assemble these entirely, refrigerate them covered overnight, and return them to room temperature right before baking.
