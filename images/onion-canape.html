🧅 Onion Canape

A classic, savory party bite featuring a rich, creamy whipped butter and mayonnaise base spread over thin bread, topped with a sweet slice of fresh onion and bubbly broiled parmesan cheese.

Prep Time Bake Time Broil Time
15 mins 15 mins 2-3 mins (until bubbly)

Nutritional Highlights

*Savory Lipid Emulsion
The classic combination of whipped real butter and mayonnaise creates a rich, smooth base coat that protects thin bread from drying out during intense baking cycles.
*Allium Antioxidants
Fresh raw onion rounds sweeten slightly as they bake, injecting powerful quercetin compounds that support arterial strength and immune defense tracks.
*Grated Parmesan Umami
A generous crowning dusting of authentic aged parmesan delivers an explosive savory finish alongside essential bone-strengthening structural calcium.

Ingredients Scaling Guide

Ingredient Name Full Batch ½ Batch ¼ Batch
Mayonnaise 2 cups 1 cup ½ cup
Butter ½ lb ½ cup (1 stick) ¼ cup (½ stick)
Parmesan Cheese 3 oz 1 ½ oz 1 oz
Base & Tops Very thin sliced bread & small fresh onion pieces

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to bake or prep your broiler element. Arrange thin bread slices onto a large, clean baking tray.
  2. In a medium bowl, blend your chosen batch size quantities of mayonnaise and butter together thoroughly until completely integrated and uniform.
  3. Spread a smooth layer of the whipped butter-mayo mixture generously across each piece of thin bread.
  4. Top each coated slice cleanly with one small round piece of fresh onion.
  5. Generously sprinkle your measured grated parmesan cheese uniformly across the top of the onion layers.
  6. To Bake: Place in the oven and bake for 15 minutes until set.
  7. To Finish: Switch the oven function over to broil for an additional 2-3 minutes, monitoring closely, until the cheese topping turns bubbly and beautifully lightly browned. Serve warm!
