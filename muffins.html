🫐 Homemade Blueberry Muffins

Fluffy, bakery-style muffins bursting with fresh blueberries and finished with a golden, sugary crumb topping.

Prep Time Bake Time Yield
15 mins 20 mins 12 muffins
Calories Protein Carbs Fats
210 kcal 4g 32g 8g

🥣 Ingredients

  • 2 cups All-Purpose Flour
  • 1 tablespoon Baking Powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon Salt
  • 1/2 cup Unsalted Butter (melted)
  • 1 cup Granulated Sugar
  • 2 Large Eggs
  • 1/2 cup Milk
  • 1 1/2 cups Fresh Blueberries

👩‍🍳 Cooking Directions

  1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a 12-cup muffin tin with paper liners.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
  3. In a separate bowl, blend the melted butter, granulated sugar, eggs, and milk until smooth.
  4. Gently fold the wet ingredients into the dry mixture until just combined (do not overmix). Gently fold in the fresh blueberries.
  5. Divide the batter evenly among the muffin cups.
  6. Bake for 20 minutes, or until the tops are golden-brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Let cool completely before serving.
