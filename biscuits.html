🫓 Protein Breakfast Biscuits

A savory, fluffy breakfast meal-prep option packed with clean lean chicken sausage, cheddar cheese, and Greek yogurt.

Prep TimeCook TimeServings
15 mins25 mins12 biscuits

Nutritional Highlights

*Greek Yogurt Base
Utilizing thick Greek yogurt significantly increases total protein density while adding a soft, tender crumb consistency to the baked biscuit framework.
*Lean Breakfast Fuel
Finely chopped chicken sausage provides 13 grams of high-yield protein per serving to maintain morning satiety and kickstart metabolism.
*Sustained Carbohydrates
Balanced flour structure provides 23 grams of muscle-fueling complex carbohydrates, ideal for long morning energy retention.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup plain 2% or whole-milk Greek yogurt (240g)
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil or melted butter
  • 2 ½ cups all-purpose flour (330g)
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • ¼ teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 ¾ cups cooked chicken breakfast sausage, finely chopped (190g)
  • 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese (112g)
  • ¼ cup green onion, thinly sliced (15g)

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper or spray lightly with cooking spray.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together the Greek yogurt, eggs, and olive oil until completely smooth.
  3. To the same bowl, add the flour, baking powder, baking soda, garlic powder, salt, and pepper over the top. Stir gently with a spatula or wooden spoon until just combined (the dough will be very thick).
  4. Add the chopped chicken sausage, cheddar cheese, and green onion. Fold until evenly distributed, being careful not to over-mix. (Note: If the dough is not coming together, gently use your hands to press and gather it lightly).
  5. Using a large cookie scoop or ¼-cup measure, drop mounds of dough onto the prepared baking sheet, spacing them about 2 inches apart to yield 12 biscuits. Lightly press any stray pieces of sausage back into the dough.
  6. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until puffed, golden around the edges, and set in the center. Let cool on the pan for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack. Enjoy!
