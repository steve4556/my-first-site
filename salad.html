🥗 Grilled Chicken Salad

A refreshing and satisfying salad that's packed with protein, flavor, and fresh vegetables.

Prep TimeCook TimeServings
15 mins10 mins2 people

Nutritional Highlights

*High Protein
Uses lean grilled chicken breast to support muscle recovery and keep you full.
*Fiber Rich
Loaded with dark leafy greens and fresh cucumbers to aid digestion.
*Healthy Fats
Finished with a drizzle of olive oil, which is excellent for heart health.

Ingredients

  • 1 grilled chicken breast, sliced
  • 3 cups mixed salad greens (spinach, romaine, or arugula)
  • 1/2 cucumber, sliced
  • 1/4 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Wash the mixed greens thoroughly and pat them dry with a paper towel.
  2. Place the greens into a large serving bowl.
  3. Toss in the sliced cucumbers and halved cherry tomatoes.
  4. Arrange the warm, sliced grilled chicken breast across the top of the vegetables.
  5. Whisk the olive oil and lemon juice together in a small cup, drizzle it over the salad, and enjoy immediately!
